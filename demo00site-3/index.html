Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Neural Vendas

How does Neural Vendas compare to human sales representatives?

Neural Vendas AI agents complement your human sales team by handling repetitive tasks like initial outreach, lead qualification, and follow-ups. This allows your human representatives to focus on high-value activities like relationship building and closing deals. Our AI agents work 24/7, never take vacations, and consistently follow your sales playbook.

How long does it take to set up and train an AI sales agent?

Most customers can set up their first AI sales agent within 1-2 days. The basic setup takes just a few hours, and then you can continue to refine and train your agent over time. Our platform includes pre-built templates and training data to help you get started quickly.

What kind of results can I expect?

Results vary by industry and use case, but our customers typically see a 2-4x increase in qualified leads, 45% reduction in sales cycle length, and 30-60% decrease in cost per acquisition. Our platform provides detailed analytics so you can track your ROI.

Which CRM systems does Neural Vendas integrate with?

Neural Vendas integrates with all major CRM platforms including Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive, and Microsoft Dynamics. We also offer an API for custom integrations with other systems.

Is my data secure with Neural Vendas?

Yes, security is our top priority. Neural Vendas is SOC 2 Type II compliant, uses end-to-end encryption for all communications, and follows industry best practices for data protection. Your data is never used to train other customers' AI agents.
