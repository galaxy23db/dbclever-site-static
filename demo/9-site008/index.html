Your Questions Answered

How quickly can I get started with Neural Vendas?

Most customers are up and running within 48 hours. Our onboarding team will guide you through the setup process, including agent creation and integration with your existing systems.

Do I need technical expertise to use Neural Vendas?

No technical expertise is required. Our no-code platform allows anyone to create and manage AI agents through an intuitive interface.

How does Neural Vendas integrate with my existing CRM?

We offer native integrations with major CRM platforms including Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive. For other systems, our API allows for custom integrations.

Is my data secure with Neural Vendas?

Absolutely. We employ enterprise-grade security measures including end-to-end encryption, regular security audits, and compliance with GDPR and other data protection regulations.

Can I customize the AI agents to match my brand voice?

Yes, our AI agents are fully customizable. You can train them using your existing sales materials and communications to ensure they accurately represent your brand voice and values.
