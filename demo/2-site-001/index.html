FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions - Neural Sales

How does Neural Vendas differ from traditional chatbots?

Neural Vendas uses advanced generative AI to create truly conversational agents that understand context, learn from interactions, and provide natural responses. Unlike traditional chatbots that follow rigid scripts, our AI agents can handle complex queries and adapt to different customer needs.

How long does it take to set up an AI sales agent?

With our no-code platform, you can have a basic AI sales agent up and running in as little as 30 minutes. For more complex implementations with custom integrations, it typically takes 1-2 days.

Can I customize the AI agent to match my brand voice?

Absolutely! You can customize the tone, language, and personality of your AI agent to align perfectly with your brand. You can also provide specific responses for common questions to ensure brand consistency.

What languages do Neural Vendas AI agents support?

Our AI agents currently support over 20 languages, including English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, and more. We're constantly adding support for additional languages.

How secure is my data with Neural Vendas?

We take data security very seriously. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. We are GDPR compliant and follow industry best practices for data protection. Your data is never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.
