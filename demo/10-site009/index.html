Your Questions Answered

How does Neural Vendas AI differ from chatbots?

+

Unlike traditional chatbots that follow rigid scripts, Neural Vendas uses advanced AI to understand context, learn from interactions, and generate natural responses that adapt to each unique conversation.

Can I integrate Neural Vendas with my existing CRM?

+

Yes! Neural Vendas integrates with major CRM platforms including Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and more. Custom integrations are available for Enterprise customers.

How long does it take to set up?

+

Basic setup can be completed in under an hour. For optimal performance, we recommend 1-2 weeks of training with your sales data and messaging. Our team provides full onboarding support.

Is my data secure?

+

Absolutely. Neural Vendas employs enterprise-grade security with end-to-end encryption, SOC 2 compliance, and GDPR-compliant data handling. Your data is never shared or used to train other systems.

Can I customize the AI's tone and messaging?

+

Yes, you have complete control over your AI agent's personality, tone, and messaging. You can create different profiles for different products, campaigns, or customer segments.
