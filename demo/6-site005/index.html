FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions - Neural Sales

How does Neural Vendas differ from traditional chatbots?

Neural Vendas uses advanced generative AI to create natural, context-aware conversations. Unlike traditional chatbots that follow rigid scripts, our AI agents can understand intent, adapt to customer needs, and handle complex scenarios.

How long does it take to set up an AI sales agent?

Most customers can set up their first AI sales agent in less than a day. Our no-code platform makes it easy to create, train, and deploy your agent. More complex setups with custom integrations may take 2-3 days.

Can I customize the AI agent to match my brand voice?

Absolutely! You can customize the tone, language, and personality of your AI agent to match your brand. You can also provide specific responses for common questions and scenarios.

What kind of results can I expect?

Our customers typically see a 30-45% increase in qualified leads, 24/7 sales coverage, and a 20-30% reduction in sales cycle time. Results vary based on your industry and implementation.

How secure is my data with Neural Vendas?

We take security seriously. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other relevant data protection regulations. We never use your customer data to train our models without explicit permission.
