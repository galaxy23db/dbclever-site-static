FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions - Neural Sales

How does the AI agent understand customer inquiries?

Our AI agents use advanced natural language processing to understand the context and intent behind customer messages. They're trained on your specific products, services, and common customer questions to provide accurate, relevant responses.

Can I customize the AI agent's personality?

Yes! You can customize the tone, style, and personality of your AI agent to match your brand voice. Whether you want professional, friendly, or enthusiastic, you can adjust these settings in the dashboard.

How do I integrate Neural Vendas with my website?

Integration is simple with our JavaScript snippet. Just copy and paste the code into your website, and the chat widget will appear. For more advanced integrations, our API documentation provides all the details you need.

What happens when the AI can't answer a question?

You can configure how your AI agent handles situations it can't resolve. Options include escalating to a human agent, collecting contact information for follow-up, or suggesting alternative resources. The AI continuously learns from these interactions to improve over time.

Is my customer data secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption for all data in transit and at rest. We're compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and other data protection regulations. Your customer data belongs to you and is never used to train other companies' AI agents.
