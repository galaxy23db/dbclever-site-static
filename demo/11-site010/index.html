Got Questions? We've Got Answers

How long does it take to set up an AI agent?

Most customers have their first agent up and running in less than 30 minutes. Our drag-and-drop interface makes it simple, and you can use our templates to get started even faster.

What's the learning curve for my team?

Neural Vendas is designed to be intuitive for anyone, regardless of technical background. Most users are comfortable with the platform after a single 30-minute onboarding session.

Can I customize the AI to match my brand voice?

Absolutely! You can customize everything from tone and language to conversation flows and responses. Your AI agents will sound exactly like your brand.

How does Neural Vendas integrate with my existing CRM?

We offer native integrations with popular CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and more. For other systems, our API makes it easy to connect and sync data automatically.

Is my data secure with Neural Vendas?

Security is our priority. We're SOC 2 compliant, use encryption for all data, and never share your information. Your data is only used to train your specific AI agents.
