FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions - Neural Vendas

How does Neural Vendas differ from traditional chatbots?

Neural Vendas uses advanced generative AI to create dynamic, contextual conversations rather than following fixed scripts. Our AI agents understand intent, learn from interactions, and provide human-like responses that traditional chatbots simply cannot match.

Do I need technical knowledge to use Neural Vendas?

Not at all! Our no-code platform is designed for business users. You can build, train, and deploy AI sales agents without any programming knowledge. Our intuitive interface makes it easy to create sophisticated AI agents in minutes.

How quickly can I get started with Neural Vendas?

Most customers are up and running within 24-48 hours. Our onboarding team will help you set up your first AI agent, integrate with your existing systems, and train it with your specific product information and sales processes.

Is Neural Vendas secure for enterprise use?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption for all data, comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other global privacy regulations, and offer SOC 2 compliance for enterprise customers. Your data security is our top priority.

Can Neural Vendas integrate with my existing CRM?

Yes, we offer seamless integration with popular CRM systems including Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, and many others. Our API also allows for custom integrations with proprietary systems.
