Got Questions? We've Got Answers

How long does it take to set up an AI sales agent?

Most customers have their first agent up and running in less than 30 minutes. Our drag-and-drop interface makes it simple to design conversation flows, and you can import existing sales materials to train your agent quickly.

What's the learning curve for using Neural Vendas?

Neural Vendas is designed to be intuitive for non-technical users. If you can use a modern CRM, you can use our platform. We also provide free onboarding sessions for all new customers to ensure you get the most out of the platform.

Can I customize my AI agent's personality and responses?

Absolutely! You have full control over your agent's tone, personality, and conversation style. You can also create custom responses for specific scenarios and train the AI on your company's unique value propositions and sales approaches.

How does Neural Vendas integrate with my existing CRM?

We offer native integrations with popular CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive. For other systems, our API and Zapier integration make it easy to connect your data. All lead information and conversation history sync automatically.

Is my data secure with Neural Vendas?

Security is our priority. We're SOC 2 compliant, use encryption for all data in transit and at rest, and never share your data with third parties. Your sales information is only used to train your specific AI agents, not shared across customers.
